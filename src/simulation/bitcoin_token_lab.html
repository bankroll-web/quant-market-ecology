Timing, model and experiment details

Trade tokens include arrivals in [previous book receipt, current book receipt). Ties at the current receipt are deferred. Late trades enter later tokens; earlier tokens are never revised. The price anchor is the book observed at decision time, and all target receipts are later. Receipt reversals, gaps and episode boundaries invalidate contexts.

This diagnostic assumes zero processing latency and inherits the frozen book reconstruction. The five recordings were already examined; they are development evidence. Only additional contiguous data and execution-cost testing can establish whether these findings generalize. Anonymous book changes do not identify retail traders, institutions or market makers.
